ACCESSIBILITY AUDIT REPORT & STATEMENT
Project: Be yoU Energy Website
Target Market: United Kingdom (UK)
Compliance Standard: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA
Last Updated: August 2026

1. CONFORMANCE STATUS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Be yoU Energy is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people 
with disabilities. We are actively working to align our web experience 
with the WCAG 2.1 Level AA standard to comply with the UK Equality Act 
2010 framework.

2. CORE ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES IMPLEMENTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Semantic HTML Structure: Our templates strictly utilise modern structural 
  elements (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <article>, and <footer>) 
  ensuring clear heading hierarchies (H1 -> H2 -> H3) for seamless screen 
  reader navigation.
* Text Alternatives (Alt Text): All functional visual anchors, product 
  thumbnails, and diagnostic graphics include informative, descriptive 
  'alt' attributes for visually impaired users.
* Language Declaration: Every page is configured with explicit regional 
  localisation (lang="en-GB") to ensure accurate synthesized speech 
  pronunciation by assistive technologies.
* Accessible Form Validation: Interactive contact fields employ explicit 
  associative text labels (<label for="...">) and conditional mandatory 
  attributes ('required') to guide screen reader focus predictably.
* Device Agnostic Layout: Built with responsive viewport parameters to 
  prevent layout breaking or text overlap when zoomed or accessed via 
  mobile devices.

3. DYNAMIC ELEMENTS & SCRIPT COMPLIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Conditional Input Fields: The witness identification module inside 
  'contact.html' dynamically alters field visibility and 'required' 
  states natively via JavaScript. Screen reader users are supported by 
  clear contextual instructions explaining why these details are requested.
* E-commerce Interaction: Cart interactions and row deletions are 
  triggered via accessible button elements operating transparently 
  with LocalStorage arrays.

4. AUDIT & TESTING METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The core pages of the Be yoU Energy ecosystem have been checked using:
* Manual keyboard-only navigation paths.
* Structural semantic markup inspection.
* Zoom factor checks (up to 200%) to ensure text scannability.

5. FEEDBACK & CONTACT ASSISTANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
We welcome feedback on the accessibility of the Be yoU Energy website. 
If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please notify us immediately 
at our contact details listed in the site footer.
