Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Be yoU Energy, we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal and bioenergetic data in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our specialised bioenergetic scans and remote radionic treatments, we process the following information when you place an order:

  • Personal Details: Your full name and date of birth, which serve as essential identifiers for energetic alignments.
  • Contact & Transaction Data: Your email address, phone number, and billing information required to process payments and send order updates.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal and bioenergetic data is used strictly to conduct your requested analysis, assemble custom radionic protocols, and deliver digital deliverables. We do not sell, rent, or share your sensitive profile with any third parties. All information is processed following our rigorous protocol and remains completely confidential.

3. Data Security

We implement advanced security measures to maintain the safety of your personal details and visual anchors. Your information is stored on secure servers and access is strictly limited to authorised processing for your bioenergetic alignments. In accordance with UK law, we retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfil your therapy protocols.

4. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a resident of the United Kingdom, you hold specific legal rights regarding your personal data. You have the right to request access to the information we hold, demand immediate corrections to any inaccuracies, or request the complete deletion of your records from our systems at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out directly through our contact details listed in the footer below.
